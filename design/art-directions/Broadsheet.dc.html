STRANGER BUYS EVERY BOARD IN ABILENE
Stock-yards rising beside the K.P. grade. — Texas herds expected by July.
Mr. J. G. McCOY, lately of Illinois, has purchased the whole stock of lumber at Abilene and set carpenters to raising pens of great extent by the railroad. He advertises in the Texas papers and declares that the longhorn will find a market here. Sober men differ on the matter.
DICKINSON COUNTY. — A petition circulates for a vote upon bonds in aid of the railroad. Its friends say the county cannot lose.
