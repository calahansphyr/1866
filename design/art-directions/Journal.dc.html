Account Book — Hollis Claim
Spring, 1867
Rode to Abilene. A man McCoy has bought every board in town and is raising cattle pens by the grade. Says Texas herds will come north in July. He wants all my hay, and will pay forty dollars for it, today.
At the saloon they say the county will vote railroad bonds. Can't lose, they say.
