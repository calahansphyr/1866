Dear Mother,
December 1867
Lucky
In the spring I put the seed on Pruitt's book instead of paying for it.
In 3 springs out of 10 like this one, the book ran past $60.
Sound
All summer I broke sod rather than hiring out to the rail crews. We have twenty-two acres now.
Sound
We sold at harvest, paid Pruitt every cent, and put up two more jars.
Unlucky
Then the hardest winter anyone remembers. The cellar ran dry in February, and $9 went back on the book.
Ten winters that might have come
Your cellar lasted the winter in 8 of them. This was one of the other 2.
The House stands at $212. We owe Pruitt $9.
Your son, J. Hollis
